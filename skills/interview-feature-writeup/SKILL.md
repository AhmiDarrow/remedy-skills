# Interview Feature Writeup

## When to use
You have a transcript and permission to publish from the conversation.

## Steps
1. Confirm what was agreed: on the record, off-limits parts, whether the subject reviews quotes, and the target length and format.
2. Remove off-record material from the working copy first so it cannot leak into the draft.
3. Tag passages by theme and mark the strongest quotes with timestamps.
4. Choose one throughline. For Q&A, reorder and merge questions only if answers keep their meaning, and note that it was edited and condensed.
5. Quote exact words for anything in quotation marks. Trim filler lightly; paraphrase when a quote needs heavy repair.
6. Keep each answer with the question that prompted it; a qualified "yes, but" must keep the "but".
7. Check names, titles, dates and checkable claims separately from the interview.
8. List quotes with timestamps and flag unclear audio for replay.

## Watch out for
- Stitching sentences from different moments into one quote.
- Trusting the transcript on proper nouns, numbers and "can" versus "can't"; verify against the recording.
- Inventing scene details for a narrative profile.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Every quoted sentence is traceable to a timestamp, off-record material is absent, and unverified claims are listed.
