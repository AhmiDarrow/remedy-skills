# Lost Phone Response

## When to use
A device is missing now. Keep instructions short and sequential.

## Steps
1. From another trusted device, the owner signs in to the official find-device service for that platform. Check location and battery, and ring it if it may be nearby.
2. If not immediately recoverable, use lost mode or remote lock with a callback message that shows an alternative contact.
3. If theft is likely, or the unlock code may have been seen, call the carrier to suspend the line and SIM, and change the main email and platform account passwords.
4. Remove or freeze payment cards in the wallet and contact banks about banking apps. Watch statements.
5. Revoke the device's sessions in email, password manager, messaging and social accounts, after confirming the owner still has another way to pass two-step checks.
6. Record IMEI from the box, bill or account, and file a police report; tell the insurer within its deadline.
7. Decide about remote erase: it protects data but may end tracking, depending on the platform.
8. If it was a work phone, tell the employer at once.

## Watch out for
- Never go alone to a located address to retrieve it; give the location to police.
- Texts claiming the phone was found with a sign-in link are a common way to steal the account and unlock it.
- Do not remove the device from the account; that can release the activation lock. Signing it out of the platform account, including through a password change or sign-out-everywhere, may also stop you locating or locking it, so locate and lock first and check the platform's guidance.
- Suspending the line cuts the phone's mobile data, which can stop location updates.
- Cancelling the number before arranging how codes will arrive.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The device is recovered or locked, line and payments are protected, sessions are handled and report references are saved.
