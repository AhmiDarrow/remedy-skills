# Employee Offboarding Plan

## When to use
Someone is leaving and work, access and obligations must not fall through.

## Steps
1. Confirm last working day, exit time, who may know, and whether access ends immediately; HR or advisers settle employment matters.
2. Inventory duties from calendars, task lists and teammates, including rare ones like annual renewals.
3. For each duty name the receiving person, documentation and a demonstration date.
4. Inventory access: identity account, mail, shared drives, finance, social accounts, domains, code, physical keys and any shared passwords.
5. Before disabling anything, transfer ownership of files, automations, subscriptions, two-factor devices and admin roles.
6. Schedule disabling and session revocation at the agreed time, and rotate shared secrets. A person with authority performs it.
7. Set up mail forwarding or auto-reply as policy and privacy law allow.
8. Track equipment, expenses, final pay items for payroll, and knowledge gaps.

## Watch out for
- Deleting the account, which can destroy files and records subject to retention.
- A domain or billing account registered to the leaver personally.
- Missing apps signed up outside the identity system.
- Announcing before the person has agreed timing.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Every duty has a recipient, every access item has an owner and removal time, and critical ownership transfers are confirmed.
