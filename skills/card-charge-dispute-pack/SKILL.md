# Card Charge Dispute Pack

## When to use
A payment should be reversed and the merchant has not fixed it, or the charge is unauthorised.

## Steps
1. Classify first. Unauthorised use goes to the issuer immediately by phone, and the card may need blocking. A quarrel over an authorised purchase is a different process.
2. Record transaction date, posted date, amount, currency, merchant descriptor and payment method. Credit, debit, bank transfer and wallet payments carry different protections.
3. Ask the issuer or read its terms for the time limit and required evidence.
4. For merchant disputes, document the attempt to resolve it directly, with dates.
5. Gather what fits the reason: expected delivery date and tracking, cancellation confirmation, agreed price, return proof, description versus photos of what arrived.
6. Write a short chronology and one paragraph stating the reason and amount, using only supportable facts.
7. Number attachments and refer to them in the text.
8. The person submits and enters any card data. Log the case number, any temporary credit and reply deadlines.

## Watch out for
- A temporary credit can be reversed if the merchant's response is accepted.
- Calling a real purchase fraud to get a faster refund is itself dishonest.
- Disputing while also accepting a merchant refund, then keeping both.
- Cancelling a card does not necessarily end a subscription or contract.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The pack has a reason, chronology and numbered evidence, is submitted inside the window, and the follow-up date is logged.
