# Parking Ticket Appeal

## When to use
A penalty notice has arrived and the person is considering challenging it.

## Steps
1. Identify the issuer: public authority, police or court, or private operator. The route and consequences differ.
2. Verify the notice is genuine through the issuer's independently found site; fake windscreen notices and payment texts exist.
3. Record deadlines for early-payment discount and each appeal stage, and whether appealing pauses or forfeits the discount.
4. Read the issuer's and local official guidance on accepted grounds.
5. Compare notice details with facts: registration, place, time, contravention code, who the keeper was.
6. Collect evidence: payment record with registration entered, permit, photos of signs and road markings from the driver's approach, loading or breakdown proof, sale documents.
7. Write one paragraph per ground, each tied to evidence. Put mitigation last and label it as a request for discretion.
8. The person submits and keeps confirmation. Note the next independent appeal level.

## Watch out for
- Ignoring a notice in the hope it lapses.
- Naming a different driver untruthfully.
- Generic internet appeal templates citing law from another country.
- Photos taken weeks later after signs changed; state the date.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The appeal states grounds with evidence and is filed before the deadline, with the cost of losing made clear beforehand.
