# Kid Device Safety Setup

## When to use
A parent is preparing a device a child will use.

## Steps
1. Note the child's age, device model and operating system version, and what it is for. Look up current official guides for that version because menus move.
2. For a used device, back up what matters, sign out the previous owner's accounts and reset it.
3. Create a proper child or supervised account linked to a parent account, with a truthful birth date. Parents type all passwords.
4. Turn on updates, screen lock, find-my-device.
5. Require approval for purchases and installs, and remove stored payment cards or lock them.
6. Review communication per app and game: who can message, friend, voice chat or see location. Check in-game settings too since system controls may not reach them.
7. Set content ratings, downtime and location sharing, explaining each to the child.
8. Teach what to do: do not reply, keep evidence, tell an adult; show block and report.
9. Test from the child's profile by trying a purchase, a blocked app and a stranger message path, then set a review date.

## Watch out for
- An adult account on the child's device bypassing all restrictions.
- Browser or web versions of apps that skip app limits.
- Treating filters as complete protection.
- If there is a sign of grooming or sexual exploitation, keep the device and messages, do not confront the person, and contact police or the official reporting body. Never copy, screenshot, forward or ask to be shown sexual images of a child; tell the authorities where they are.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Tests from the child's profile confirm purchase, install and contact limits, and the child knows how to report.
