# Usability Test Plan

## When to use
A team wants to watch people use a design and learn what to change.

## Steps
1. Name the decisions at stake and turn them into research questions. If the question is demand or preference, say a usability test will not answer it.
2. Choose method: moderated or unmoderated, diagnostic (find problems) or measurement (quantify performance). Measurement needs a larger sample and fixed conditions.
3. Define participants by behaviour and experience relevant to the tasks, include people with disabilities or assistive technology where relevant, and write a screener that does not reveal the wanted answers. For finding problems, small rounds of about five per distinct group with fixes in between are a common start, not a guarantee.
4. Write tasks as realistic goals with context, avoiding words on the interface. Define the start point and what counts as success, partial and failure.
5. Order tasks to limit learning effects and keep the session within the time, with the most important early.
6. List what to record: completion, assists, errors, hesitations, quotes. Use timing only without think-aloud interruptions and with an adequate sample.
7. Script welcome, consent (recording, use, storage, deletion, right to stop), "we test the design, not you", think-aloud practice, tasks, neutral probes and debrief. Include when to give help.
8. Prepare test data using invented accounts, check the prototype's dead ends, run a pilot and fix the script.
9. Set a note template: participant code, task, observation, evidence.
10. Synthesise by grouping problems, rating severity from impact and recurrence, separating observation from explanation, and assigning action and retest.

## Watch out for
- "Would you click here?" or explaining the interface when someone struggles.
- Reporting percentages from five people.
- Testing colleagues who built or know the product.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The plan has questions, screener, tasks with success criteria, moderator script with consent, data handling, pilot step and synthesis template, ready to run or with blockers named.
