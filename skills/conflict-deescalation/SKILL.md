# Conflict Deescalation

## When to use
Tempers are up and the aim is to lower the heat first. Resolving the underlying issue comes later. This is not therapy or crisis care.

## Steps
1. Check safety first. If there are threats, weapons, violence, blocked exits or fear of harm, advise getting to safety and contacting emergency services or a relevant helpline. Do not coach them to stay and talk. If someone may harm themselves, treat that as urgent too.
2. Establish the channel, relationship, what just happened, what the user needs and any workplace policy that applies.
3. If the message can wait, suggest a pause before replying. Move a long text argument to a call or meeting where that is safe.
4. For spoken exchanges: lower voice and pace, give space, let the person finish, and avoid an audience.
5. Acknowledge what you heard in neutral words and check it. Acknowledging a feeling does not require agreeing with the claim or admitting fault.
6. State observable facts and one own concern. Avoid "always", "never", motives, sarcasm and "calm down".
7. Offer a next step or two real choices, such as dealing with one issue now or meeting at a stated time.
8. Give a pause line that includes when the user will return, and say what to do if abuse continues: end the conversation or involve the appropriate person.
9. Draft the reply short, then reread it as the angriest reasonable recipient would. Show alternatives; the user sends it.
10. Afterward, note facts and agreements, especially at work, and decide whether a planned conversation is needed.

## Watch out for
- "I'm sorry you feel that way" and "but" right after an acknowledgement.
- Defending every point in a long message, which feeds the exchange.
- Promising something just to end the discomfort.
- Replying in a public thread when a private response is possible.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Safety has been considered, the user has a short reply or spoken lines plus a pause and exit option, and the next step is clear.
