# Appliance Repair Triage

## When to use
An appliance misbehaves and the person wants to know what to check, and whether to repair or replace.

## Steps
1. Stop rules first: burning smell, scorch marks, tripping breaker, gas smell, sparking or water reaching a plug means unplug if safe and call a professional. With gas smell, leave and call the gas emergency line from outside.
2. Get the exact model and serial number from the rating plate. Search the maker's site for the manual, error code table and any recall or safety notice for that model.
3. Record the symptom precisely: when in the cycle, error code, sounds, what changed recently.
4. Check external causes: power at the socket (test with another device), door latch, child lock or delay start, water supply tap, kinked hose, tripped residual-current device.
5. Check user-serviceable parts named in the manual only: drain filter, spray arms, lint filter, condenser coils, door seals. Unplug first and have towels ready before opening a drain filter.
6. Check warranty, extended cover and proof of purchase before anyone opens the casing.
7. For a repair-or-replace view, put side by side: repair quote including call-out, appliance age, part availability, repeat failures, replacement cost with fitting and disposal. Present it without deciding.

## Watch out for
- Do not guide anyone into mains wiring, microwave internals or sealed refrigerant circuits; stored charge and refrigerants are hazardous even unplugged.
- Gas parts, burners and gas connections on cookers or dryers are for a registered gas engineer only.
- Generic error codes from forums; the same code means different things across models.
- Resetting repeatedly to force a cycle through a real fault.
- Ordering a part by appearance and not by model number.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The fault is fixed, or a booking note exists with model, symptom, checks already done and warranty status.
