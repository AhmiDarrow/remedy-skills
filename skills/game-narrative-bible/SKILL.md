# Game Narrative Bible

## When to use
A game world needs one reference so content stays consistent.

## Steps
1. Gather existing scripts, item text, design notes and shipped content. Shipped content usually outranks drafts; confirm the canon order with the user.
2. Record each fact with its source and status: canon, proposed, deprecated, or deliberately unknown. Do not merge contradictions silently.
3. Write the premise and hard rules (magic, technology, death, travel) with costs, limits and known exceptions. Tie rules that players act on to the actual game mechanics.
4. For each main character give want, need, fear, relationships, secrets, arc by chapter, and voice: sentence habits, things never said, three sample lines.
5. Build a timeline with absolute order, then factions with goals, resources, conflicts and what ordinary members believe.
6. Track knowledge: who learns which secret when, and when the player does. Do this per branch.
7. Describe tone with do and avoid examples in your own words, plus content limits for the intended rating.
8. Keep a glossary with spelling, pronunciation and capitalisation, noting terms that need translator guidance.
9. Put a one-page summary first, mark spoiler sections, and index by stable names.
10. Add a pre-merge checklist for new content and an owner and process for changing canon.

## Watch out for
- Hundreds of pages of history nobody consults; detail what content touches.
- Explaining mysteries the story wants open without labelling them writer-only.
- Choices that kill a character while later content assumes they live.
- Filling gaps with inventions marked as established fact.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
A new writer can find a rule, voice, date or spelling quickly, each fact has a status, and found contradictions are listed for decision.
