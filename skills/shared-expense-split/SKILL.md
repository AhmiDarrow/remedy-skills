# Shared Expense Split

## When to use
A flat, trip or group purchase has uneven payments to reconcile.

## Steps
1. Agree the rules before computing: equal shares, by income, by usage or per item; who joined late; whether deposits and gifts count.
2. Build a ledger with date, payer, amount, currency, description and the people each expense covers.
3. Convert other currencies with one agreed method, such as the rate the payer's card actually charged.
4. Compute each person's share of each expense in the smallest currency unit, and assign leftover cents by a stated rule so shares sum exactly.
5. Net balance for each person equals paid minus owed. Check all net balances add to zero.
6. Settle by repeatedly matching the largest debtor to the largest creditor, which keeps the number of transfers low.
7. Show each person their own line items so they can check them.
8. Record settlements as separate entries when confirmed. The people make their own payments.

## Watch out for
- Charging everyone for items only some used, such as drinks.
- Counting a repayment as a new expense.
- Refunds and returned deposits that need to flow back to the original sharers.
- Changing the rule after seeing who it favours.
