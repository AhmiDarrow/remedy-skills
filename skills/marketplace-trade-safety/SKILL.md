# Marketplace Trade Safety

## When to use
Someone is about to sell or buy from a stranger, or the other side is proposing something odd.

## Steps
1. Establish side (buyer or seller), item, value, platform, delivery or collection and proposed payment.
2. Read the platform's current protection terms: which payment methods and handover types are covered.
3. Keep chat and payment on the platform. Treat a push to another app, an emailed payment notice or a courier link as warning signs.
4. Sellers: confirm money in their own account or app, opened by themselves, before releasing goods. A screenshot or email is not payment. Refuse overpayment with a request to refund the difference.
5. Buyers: check the item works and matches before paying; for phones check activation lock is removed, and for items with serial numbers check ownership proof and any stolen or finance register that exists.
6. Meet in a busy daytime place, take someone along for valuables, and do not hand over identity documents.
7. For shipping, use tracked delivery to the platform's recorded address with proof of contents and signature where value warrants.
8. Save listing, chat, serial number and receipt. Wipe personal data from sold devices.

## Watch out for
- "To receive the money, enter your card details here" is theft.
- Verification codes requested "to prove you are real" can hijack accounts or numbers.
- Reversible payment methods used on collection.
- Deals that leave protection in exchange for a small discount.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Payment method and handover are agreed within platform protection, and funds or item condition are verified before exchange.
