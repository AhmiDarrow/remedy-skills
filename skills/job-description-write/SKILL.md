# Job Description Write

## When to use
A hiring manager needs a posting, or an old one attracts the wrong applicants.

## Steps
1. Ask what this person must have achieved after about six and twelve months, and why the role is open.
2. Derive the main responsibilities from those outcomes with a rough time split.
3. Split must-haves from learnable extras. Test each must-have: would you reject an otherwise excellent candidate without it?
4. Replace proxies such as years or degrees with demonstrable ability unless truly or legally required.
5. State level, manager, team, location or remote limits, hours, travel and on-call honestly.
6. Include pay range and benefits as approved; check pay-transparency and posting rules for each location.
7. Describe the hiring stages, time expected and how to request accommodation.
8. Remove age-coded or physical wording not tied to the job, and have HR or counsel review.

## Watch out for
- A wish list describing three jobs.
- Invented salary, perks or culture claims.
- "Digital native", "young team" and similar.
- Requiring more years with a tool than makes sense for it.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Every must-have ties to an outcome, conditions and process are stated, and unconfirmed items are marked for the owner.
