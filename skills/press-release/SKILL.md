# Press Release

## When to use
An organisation has news it wants reporters to be able to cover accurately.

## Steps
1. Ask what changed, for whom, why now and why someone outside the organisation would care. If there is no news, say so and suggest a better format.
2. Collect source documents for dates, numbers, availability, price, names and partners. Confirm who must approve, including any named third party.
3. Settle timing: immediate release or an embargo with exact date, time and zone. An embargo works only if recipients agree to it. Listed companies, investment news and regulated claims need legal or compliance review before anyone outside sees it.
4. Write a factual headline and optional subhead without hype.
5. Open with a dateline and a first paragraph that can stand alone: who, what, when, where and why it matters.
6. Order the rest by importance, so it can be cut from the bottom: key details, evidence, availability, context.
7. Add one or two quotes that give judgement or perspective, not restated features. Label drafted quotes pending until the named person approves the exact words.
8. Finish with a short boilerplate, media contact, and links to images with captions, credits and usage permission.
9. Check every claim against sources, mark unconfirmed items, and give the user the draft and approval list. The user distributes it.

## Watch out for
- "Leading", "first" or "revolutionary" without proof.
- Forward-looking promises nobody has committed to.
- Pre-announcing a partner before written approval.
- Placeholder contacts or test links left in the final.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The lead states the news plainly, all facts trace to sources, quotes and third-party names show approval status, and release timing and contact are set.
