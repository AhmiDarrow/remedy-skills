# Video Script

## When to use
A video of a few minutes or more needs words and pictures planned together before filming. For clips of about a minute built for phones, a short-form approach fits better.

## Steps
1. Settle audience, the one thing they should understand or do, target length, format, presenter, and what can really be filmed or captured.
2. Check facts with `web.search` and `web.fetch` or the user's material. Keep a numbered source list and tag each factual line.
3. Outline as questions the viewer will have, in order. Give each section a time budget that sums to the target.
4. Open within the first several seconds on the problem or result and say what the video will deliver. Move introductions and channel business later or drop them.
5. Write two columns per row: Visual and Audio, with a time estimate. Estimate speech at about 130 to 160 words per minute and add time for demonstrations and pauses.
6. Write for speaking: short sentences, one idea each, contractions where natural. Mark pronunciation of unusual terms.
7. In the visual column name the actual shot, screen action, diagram or B-roll that proves or clarifies the line, and mark who must produce it.
8. Add a short bridge between sections that raises the next question. Recap only where viewers need it.
9. Close with the payoff and one next action. If an end screen is used, leave a clean stretch for it.
10. Total the timings, then produce a list of shots and assets to capture.

## Watch out for
- Narration that reads out the slide word for word.
- "As you can see" with nothing planned on screen.
- Word count alone understates tutorials, where actions take longer than talk.
- Demonstration steps written without running them; mark untested ones.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Every row has both picture and audio with timing, the total is within the target, claims point to sources, and unfilmed assets and unverified steps are listed.
