# Sponsorship Creator Brief

## When to use
Money or free product is changing hands for content.

## Steps
1. Record the campaign goal, audience, and why this creator fits, based on their recent work.
2. List deliverables precisely: format, count, length, platform, publish window and how long it must stay up.
3. Give key facts and the claims allowed, each with substantiation, and claims that are forbidden. Leave the creative approach to the creator.
4. Require clear disclosure of the paid relationship, placed where viewers notice it, using the platform's label too where offered. Check current advertising rules for the audience's countries.
5. State review rounds and turnaround. Review may correct facts and safety, not script a fake personal opinion.
6. Specify usage rights: where the brand may reuse content, for how long, paid promotion rights and exclusivity.
7. State fee, payment timing, cancellation terms, and the performance data to be shared.
8. Mark contract terms for legal review and hand the draft to the user to send.

## Watch out for
- Asking for undisclosed endorsements or reviews of a product the creator never tried; refuse.
- Assuming payment includes perpetual reuse or ads.
- Hashtag disclosure buried among other tags.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Deliverables, allowed claims, disclosure, approval, rights and payment are each stated unambiguously.
