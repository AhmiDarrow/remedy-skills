# Home Emergency Sheet

## When to use
Anyone in the house, including a guest or sitter, should be able to act in an emergency without asking.

## Steps
1. Start with the local emergency number for the country, the full address as a dispatcher needs it, and directions for access (gate code held separately, floor, entrance).
2. For water, gas and electricity, record where each shut-off is, how it turns, and any tool needed. Ask the person to go and look; ask for photos. Never guess a location.
3. Add utility emergency lines copied from bills or official sites, building manager, insurer with policy number, a nearby trusted contact and an out-of-area contact.
4. Add two exits from sleeping areas where possible, one outdoor meeting point, and who helps children, anyone with limited mobility, and pets.
5. Include only the medical facts a responder would need and the household agrees to display, such as severe allergies. Keep fuller details elsewhere.
6. Keep it to one printable page in large text, with shut-offs and numbers at the top.
7. Save it to a file, suggest a printed copy near the exit or fuse box plus one on each adult's phone, and set a yearly review that includes checking the water valve.

## Watch out for
- A digital-only sheet is useless with a dead phone or power cut.
- A water valve may be seized: turn it gently, never force it, and get a plumber if it is stuck or leaks. Only locate the gas shut-off; do not operate it as a test, because appliances may then need a technician to restore safely. The same applies to the main electrical switch if anyone depends on powered medical equipment.
- Alarm codes and key hiding places do not belong on a displayed page.
- Out-of-date neighbour numbers; verify each at review.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
A one-page sheet has verified shut-off locations and numbers, no blanks presented as facts, and a review date.
