# Car Accident Record

## When to use
A collision or vehicle damage has just happened or needs documenting. Give short instructions one at a time if they are at the scene.

## Steps
1. Safety first: injuries mean calling emergency services. Move people out of traffic, use hazard lights and warning equipment where safe, and do not move an injured person unless in immediate danger.
2. Check local duties: stop, exchange details, call police when required. Leaving can be an offence.
3. Exchange names, contacts, insurer and registration numbers. Get witness contacts before they leave.
4. Photograph wide scene, vehicle positions if safe, road markings, signs, damage on all vehicles, plates, weather and light.
5. Save dashcam footage before it overwrites.
6. Write a factual account soon after: time, place, direction, approximate speed, signals, sequence. No fault conclusions.
7. Notify the insurer within the policy's time, even if not claiming when the policy requires it.
8. Log references, tow location, repair estimates, expenses and any symptoms; tell the person to see a clinician if they feel unwell, including later.

## Watch out for
- Admitting fault or speculating at the scene.
- Unsolicited tow trucks or claims firms appearing or calling; confirm they come from the insurer.
- Cash settlements before damage is known.
- Driving a car with possibly damaged lights, wheels or cooling.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Parties, witnesses, photos and account are saved together and the insurer has been notified with a reference.
