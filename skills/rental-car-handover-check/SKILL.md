# Rental Car Handover Check

## When to use
A hire car is being collected, returned, or has produced a surprise charge.

## Steps
1. Before pickup, read the booking: driver age and licence requirements, extra drivers, deposit or hold, card type accepted, mileage cap, fuel or charge policy, cross-border rules, insurance excess.
2. List cover already held through travel insurance or a card, with its conditions, so the counter offer can be judged.
3. At pickup, check that existing damage on the form matches the car. Have missing marks added and signed.
4. Take a timestamped video all round, then close photos of wheels, tyres, windscreen, roof, bumpers, interior, dashboard mileage and fuel or battery level.
5. Confirm fuel type from the cap or paperwork, how to report a breakdown, and the emergency number.
6. At return, repeat the photos, keep the final fuel receipt, and get a signed inspection. For key drop returns, photograph the parked car and location.
7. Check card statements for some weeks. For a charge, request itemised evidence, inspection time and invoice, and compare with the photos.
8. Draft the dispute with references; the renter sends it.

## Watch out for
- Dim garage photos that show nothing; use light and get close.
- Declined extras appearing on the agreement anyway; read before signing.
- Tolls and fines with admin fees arriving much later.
- Misfuelling: do not start the engine or switch on the ignition; call the rental company.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Pickup and return condition are recorded with times, and any extra charge is matched to evidence or disputed in writing.
