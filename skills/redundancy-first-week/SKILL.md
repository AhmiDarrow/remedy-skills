# Redundancy First Week

## When to use
A job is ending involuntarily and the person needs a calm plan.

## Steps
1. Acknowledge the shock, then ask country, notice date, last day, and what they have been given or asked to sign.
2. Advise not signing on the spot. Find the response deadline and any review or withdrawal period, and recommend an employment adviser, union or free advice service for agreements.
3. Gather contract, handbook, severance terms, recent payslips, bonus and equity documents, leave balance.
4. Prepare written questions: final pay date, leave payout, benefits end date, pension, equity deadlines, reference, equipment, repayment clauses.
5. From official sources, check unemployment benefit eligibility, when to apply and effects of severance on timing. Note health cover options and their deadlines.
6. Save personal documents they are entitled to, such as payslips and tax forms. Do not take company data.
7. Move personal accounts off the work email and phone number before access ends.
8. List essential outgoings and income dates, and contact creditors early if there is a gap. Add every deadline to the calendar.

## Watch out for
- Missing benefit or health cover windows while focused on job hunting.
- Copying work files, which can forfeit severance or breach the contract.
- Posting about the employer before reading confidentiality terms.
- Time limits for legal claims can be short and may run during negotiation.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
All deadlines are on the calendar, documents are collected, and agreement questions are with an adviser or listed for one.
