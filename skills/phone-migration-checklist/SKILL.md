# Phone Migration Checklist

## When to use
A person is changing phones. The old one must stay intact until the new one is proven.

## Steps
1. Note both systems and versions, free storage, SIM or eSIM, and read the official transfer guide for that pairing.
2. Update and back up the old phone, then check the backup's date and what it excludes.
3. List what often does not transfer: authenticator codes, banking and ID app enrolment, chat history, passkeys or work profiles, files stored only inside apps, downloaded media, health data, wallet cards.
4. For each authenticator entry, use the app's own export or enrol the new phone on each service while the old one still works. Confirm recovery codes are saved.
5. Run each chat app's own transfer or backup process and note encryption passwords, which the person keeps and types.
6. Check photos exist in full resolution somewhere independent of the phone.
7. If a watch is paired, follow the maker's steps for moving it, which may need doing during setup or before the old phone is erased. Transfer, then move the number with the carrier. Test calls, SMS, mobile data and messaging registration.
8. Open every important app and sign in on the new phone while the old one is still available.
9. After a few days, sign out, remove activation lock, erase the old phone and remove SIM and memory card.

## Watch out for
- Wiping or trading in before authenticator codes are tested.
- Messages tied to a number or platform that need turning off when changing systems.
- eSIM transfer needing the carrier and a network.
- Optimised photo storage meaning originals are not on the device.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Critical apps and two-step codes work on the new phone and the old one is erased only after that.
