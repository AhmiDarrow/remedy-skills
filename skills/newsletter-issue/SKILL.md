# Newsletter Issue

## When to use
A recurring newsletter needs its next issue written and checked.

## Steps
1. Read recent issues for voice, structure, send rhythm and what readers replied to or clicked. Confirm the list is made of people who opted in.
2. Gather this issue's items and choose one lead. Cut anything included only to fill a slot; a short issue is fine.
3. Pick the single primary action. Keep secondary links visibly secondary.
4. Open with the lead's value, not greetings and housekeeping. Give each section a clear heading and enough substance to be useful without clicking.
5. Offer two or three truthful subject lines and preview text that adds to the subject rather than repeating it.
6. Disclose sponsorships, affiliate links and paid placements beside them, in terms readers understand; check rules that apply to the sender.
7. Prepare the plain-text version, descriptive link text and alt text, and ensure the message makes sense with images blocked.
8. Check that unsubscribe, sender identity and required address are present through the sending service, that merge fields have fallbacks, and that every final link opens.
9. Have the user send a test to their own inboxes, check mobile and dark display, confirm audience segment and time, and send it themselves.

## Watch out for
- A sent email cannot be corrected; verify dates, prices and codes against sources.
- Private preview links or internal notes left in the body.
- "Re:" or fake urgency in subjects.
- Treating open rate as proof of reading; mail privacy features distort it.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The draft, subject options, preview text and plain-text version are ready, links and facts are checked, and the remaining pre-send checks are listed for the user.
