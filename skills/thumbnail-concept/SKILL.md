# Thumbnail Concept

## When to use
Someone needs thumbnail directions a designer can build, or wants to improve a weak one.

## Steps
1. Get the title, what the content actually delivers, audience, channel style, available photos or footage, and required aspect ratio and size. Check the platform's current upload specs.
2. Name the one reason to click. The thumbnail and title should work as a pair and not repeat the same words.
3. Draft three concepts that differ in idea, not just colour: for example the finished result, a before-and-after contrast, and a person reacting to the real subject.
4. For each, specify focal subject, crop, placement, background treatment, text, colours and the source of every image.
5. Keep to one focal point and about four words of text or fewer, in a heavy face. No text is a valid choice.
6. Separate subject from background by lightness, not hue alone. View it in greyscale to confirm.
7. Keep essentials away from edges and corners where duration badges or progress bars overlay; the bottom right is a common one.
8. Test at the smallest display size, about the width of a thumb, beside neighbouring thumbnails, in light and dark surroundings.
9. If the platform offers comparison testing, plan it with variants differing in one main idea. Otherwise change once, then compare click-through and watch time over similar periods.

## Watch out for
- Showing something that is not in the content: it earns clicks and quick exits, and may break platform rules.
- Using a real person's face, a logo or found images without permission.
- Calling a winner from a handful of impressions, or from periods with different traffic sources.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Three buildable concepts are written with image sources and text, each has been judged at small size for legibility and honesty, and a recommendation and comparison plan are stated.
