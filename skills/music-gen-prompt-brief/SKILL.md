# Music Gen Prompt Brief

## When to use
Stock tracks do not fit and the music must serve a cut or scene.

## Steps
1. Define the job: under speech, intro sting, loop or trailer, and the exact duration.
2. Note edit points in seconds and what should happen musically at each.
3. Specify tempo range, metre if it matters, instrumentation, energy curve and what to avoid.
4. To cut on beats, compute them: seconds per beat = 60 / BPM. Pick a tempo that lands key moments near bar lines.
5. For speech beds ask for sparse midrange and no vocals or lead melody.
6. Describe style by traits, not "sounds like" a named artist or song. Supply only lyrics the user owns.
7. Generate or request several short drafts and judge them under the actual picture or voice.
8. Check endings, loop seam, artefacts and level against dialogue.
9. Save source, date, license terms, commercial-use permission and prompt with the file.

## Watch out for
- Assuming generated or "royalty-free" means no conditions or no platform rights claims.
- Fading out mid-phrase instead of asking for a real ending or cutting at a bar.
- Judging music solo when it must sit under a voice.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The chosen track matches duration and hit points in context, speech stays intelligible, and its license record is saved.
