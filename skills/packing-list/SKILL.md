# Packing List

## When to use
A trip is coming and forgetting or overpacking would hurt.

## Steps
1. Get destination, dates, travellers, purpose, activities, dress codes, lodging, laundry access and transport.
2. Check the forecast near departure, or typical seasonal conditions if it is far off, and say which you used. Note rain, night temperature and altitude.
3. Check luggage size, weight and number for each carrier and fare, including the return and small connecting aircraft.
4. List what cannot be replaced first: ID and documents, payment, medicines in original packaging with extra days, glasses, keys, device essentials. These go in cabin or personal luggage.
5. Count clothes from days between washes plus a spare, using layers and pieces that combine. Base shoes on activities and wear the bulkiest while travelling.
6. Apply flight rules: spare lithium batteries and power banks go in cabin baggage only, never checked, including if a cabin bag is taken at the gate. Capacity, quantity and in-use limits vary, with airline approval needed for larger batteries. Check current airline and security rules for these, liquids and blades.
7. Check plug type and whether each device accepts the local voltage; an adapter does not convert voltage.
8. Check what lodging or organisers provide, and import rules for medicines and food.
9. Output a checklist per person, split into wear, cabin and checked, with last-minute items separate.

## Watch out for
- A generic list that ignores the activities; each special item should trace to one.
- Medicines or valuables in checked bags.
- No space left for what comes back.

## Operating rules

## Done when
A per-traveller checklist fits the luggage limits, essentials are in carried bags, battery and restricted items are placed correctly, and rules still to verify are marked.
