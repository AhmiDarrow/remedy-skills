# Job Offer Comparison

## When to use
A person has an offer in hand and wants to see it clearly. The choice is theirs.

## Steps
1. Before comparing, have them rank what matters and name deal-breakers.
2. Extract from written offers and contracts. Mark anything only said aloud as unconfirmed.
3. Break pay into base, guaranteed extras, target or discretionary bonus, equity, employer retirement contribution. Keep uncertain items out of the guaranteed total. For equity, record type, vesting and exit conditions and leave valuation and tax to a professional.
4. Add benefits and costs: insurance and waiting period, leave, commute time and money, equipment, childcare, relocation.
5. Compare working terms: hours, on-call, remote policy and where it is written, probation, notice.
6. Flag clauses for legal advice: non-compete, repayment of bonuses or training, invention ownership, side work limits.
7. Check the exploding deadline and draft a polite request for time or written clarification.
8. Show a table with unknowns visible, scored against their ranking.

## Watch out for
- Comparing monthly with annual figures, or different currencies and living costs.
- Resigning before the offer is written and conditions such as checks are cleared.
- A weighted score that hides a deal-breaker.
- Current-employer bonuses, vesting or repayment lost by leaving.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Offers sit side by side with guaranteed and uncertain value separated, open questions listed and priorities applied.
