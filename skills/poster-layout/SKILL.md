# Poster Layout

## When to use
A single sheet or screen must be noticed and understood quickly by someone passing by.

## Steps
1. Ask where it hangs, how far away readers are, how long they look, the final size and orientation, whether it will also be used digitally, and the one action wanted. A conference research poster is read close up and carries more detail, so treat it as such.
2. Gather exact copy and verify names, date, weekday, time, place, price and link against each other and the calendar.
3. Rank content in three levels: the thing that stops people, what and when and where, then the rest. Cut or move extras to a landing page.
4. Choose one dominant element (image or headline) and give it clear priority in size and space.
5. Set a grid with generous margins and consistent alignment. Group related facts so the date is never separated from its time.
6. Size type for the distance. A rough start is at least 1 cm of capital height per 3 m of viewing distance for essential text, with the headline far larger; confirm with a test print viewed from the real distance.
7. Keep strong contrast, calm the area behind text, and limit type families to one or two.
8. Place logos and sponsors in a quiet zone with their clear space.
9. For a QR code, use high contrast with a quiet border, size it for scanning distance, print the short URL too, and have it scanned from a proof.
10. Provide a layout sketch in zones with measurements, then note size-specific variants and hand off to print preflight. Provide the same information as accessible text wherever it is posted online.

## Watch out for
- Scaling one layout to other proportions without rearranging.
- Key details at the bottom edge where frames, crowds or furniture hide them.
- Images or fonts without the right licence, or too low in resolution at final size.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
The layout spec names the message and reading order, zones and type sizes for the stated size and distance, verified copy, and a proof test that is done or listed as pending.
