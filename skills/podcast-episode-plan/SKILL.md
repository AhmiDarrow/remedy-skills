# Podcast Episode Plan

## When to use
An episode needs preparation before recording.

## Steps
1. State who the episode is for and what they will understand or enjoy by the end, in one sentence. Note show format, usual length and recent episodes to avoid repeats.
2. Build a timed rundown where each segment has its purpose, key points and spoken transition. Mark what to cut if running long.
3. Script only the cold open, introduction, any sponsor read with its disclosure, and close. Use prompts elsewhere so speech stays natural. State the promise within the first minute.
4. Research facts to be mentioned and keep sources in a fact sheet. For guests, verify name pronunciation, title and biography, and share topics, timing and logistics.
5. Confirm recording and publishing consent, editing terms and rights to music or clips.
6. Check the setup: correct input selected, headphones for everyone, quiet room, notifications off, power and disk space. Record separate tracks where possible and keep a backup recording.
7. Make a short test, listen back for clipping, echo and noise, and fix at the source. Leave a few seconds of room tone.
8. During recording, note timestamps for fixes and clip candidates.
9. Prepare outputs: title, description, links promised aloud, chapter times taken from the final edit, transcript review and clip list. The user publishes.

## Watch out for
- Laptop microphone silently selected instead of the intended one.
- A rundown so detailed that nobody listens to each other.
- Remote guests relying only on the call's compressed recording.

## Done when
The rundown fits the target length, guest and rights items are confirmed or flagged, the recording checklist is ready, and show note fields are prepared.
