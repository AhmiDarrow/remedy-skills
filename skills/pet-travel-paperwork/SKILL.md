# Pet Travel Paperwork

## When to use
A pet is going on a trip with entry or carrier requirements.

## Steps
1. Note species, breed, age, weight, microchip, vaccination dates, origin, destination, transits, dates and transport.
2. Read the official import rules of the destination and each transit, and the rules for returning home. Save source and date.
3. Extract the required sequence: chip, vaccination, blood test, waiting period, parasite treatment, health certificate, government endorsement, permit, quarantine, arrival point. Order matters; a vaccine given before chipping may not count.
4. Work backwards from travel; some sequences take months. If dates cannot work, say so early.
5. Check carrier limits on breed, temperature, crate size, cabin or hold and booking quotas.
6. Book a vet who can issue the needed certificate. Leave fitness to travel and sedation to the vet.
7. Add reminders for each time window and a crate acclimatisation plan.
8. Assemble originals in one folder with copies.

## Watch out for
- A health certificate valid only for a short window before arrival.
- Breed restrictions applying even in transit.
- Short-nosed animals refused by some carriers.
- Copying another traveller's list instead of the current official one.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
A dated sequence satisfies every border and carrier rule from a cited source, with appointments placed inside their windows.
