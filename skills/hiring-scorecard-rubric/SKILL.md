# Hiring Scorecard Rubric

## When to use
Several people will assess candidates and the team wants comparable evidence.

## Steps
1. Pick four to six criteria from the role's outcomes, each observable.
2. Assign each to a particular stage and interviewer so coverage is complete without repetition.
3. Write the same core questions or work sample for every candidate, with allowed follow-ups.
4. For each criterion describe weak, adequate and strong evidence in behaviour terms.
5. Keep work samples short and relevant; disclose time and format, and offer accommodations.
6. Require notes quoting what the candidate said or did, then a rating, submitted before discussion.
7. Provide "not assessed" as an option.
8. In debrief, compare evidence per criterion and record the decision reason. Follow local record retention and privacy rules.

## Watch out for
- "Culture fit" with no behavioural definition.
- Questions about protected characteristics, family plans or health.
- Averaging scores so a failed essential criterion disappears.
- Having software or an assistant rank or reject people; humans decide.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Each criterion has a stage, owner, question and anchored levels, and scoring happens independently before debrief.
