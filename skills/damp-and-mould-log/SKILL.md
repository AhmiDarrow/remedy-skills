# Damp And Mould Log

## When to use
Mould or damp is recurring and someone needs to find the pattern or get another party to act.

## Steps
1. If anyone in the home has breathing trouble, is very young, elderly or immunocompromised, say that a clinician should be told. Do not assess health effects yourself.
2. Map each patch by room and wall, with dated photos taken from the same spot including a tape measure.
3. Note position clues: cold corners and behind furniture suggest condensation; a tide line rising from the floor suggests ground moisture; a patch that follows rain suggests penetration; one near pipes suggests a leak. Call these hypotheses, not a diagnosis.
4. If a hygrometer is available, log humidity and temperature morning and evening for two weeks, with notes on drying laundry, cooking, showers and ventilation.
5. Check the cheap items: extractor fans actually move air (a thin sheet of paper should hold against the grille), trickle vents are open, gutters are not overflowing.
6. Large areas, soft plaster, rot or suspected contaminated water call for a professional rather than cleaning.
7. Draft the report: locations, first noticed date, readings, photos, steps taken, request for inspection, and a reply date. Show it for approval before `mail.send`; keep the sent copy.
8. Keep a timeline of every response and visit. For legal duties and deadlines, point to a tenant advice service or the local housing authority.

## Watch out for
- Painting over mould before it is documented or the moisture source is fixed.
- Assuming it is all lifestyle, or all building fault, from one photo.
- Phone calls with no written follow-up leave nothing to show later.
- Do not advise withholding rent; that can have serious consequences.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
A dated map, photo set and log exist, and a report has been sent with approval or saved ready with a follow-up date.
