# Rental Move In Evidence

## When to use
A tenant is about to move in, or just has, and the starting condition needs proof.

## Steps
1. Read the lease and the landlord's inventory first. Note the deadline for returning comments and where the deposit is to be held.
2. Photograph before furniture arrives. Work room by room in one fixed order: door, each wall, floor, ceiling, windows, fittings.
3. For each defect take a wide shot showing the room and a close shot with something for scale. Open ovens, cupboards, appliance doors and under sinks.
4. Record one continuous video walkthrough running taps, flushing toilets and testing sockets, lights, heating and appliances.
5. Photograph every meter with its serial number and reading, and count all keys and fobs.
6. Build a table: room, item, condition, photo filename, matches inventory (yes, no, missing). Write specific entries such as "10 cm scratch on worktop left of hob".
7. Draft a cover note disputing inaccurate inventory lines and listing repairs needed. The person sends it within the deadline.
8. Keep original files unedited in two places, and save proof of when the note was sent.
9. Check how the deposit protection rule works locally through an official source and record the reference once issued.

## Watch out for
- Signing an inventory that says "good condition" throughout without annotating it.
- Messaging apps that compress photos and strip capture times; keep the originals.
- Reporting a safety fault only inside the inventory. Report it separately and immediately.
- Cleanliness is easy to forget and commonly argued over, so photograph it too.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Every room and meter is photographed, discrepancies are sent in writing before the deadline, and originals are backed up.
