# Lost Luggage Claim

## When to use
A checked bag is missing or damaged.

## Steps
1. If still at the airport, tell the traveller to file at the airline's baggage desk before leaving and get the report reference.
2. Keep boarding pass, bag tag stub, booking reference and photos of any damage including the tag.
3. Give a delivery address and phone that stay valid for several days, and describe the outside of the bag plus a distinctive item inside.
4. Separate urgent needs: medicine goes to a pharmacist or clinician at once. For other essentials, ask the airline what it reimburses, buy reasonably and keep receipts.
5. Look up the written claim deadlines for damage and delay in the airline's conditions and applicable convention or law; they can be very short and the airport report may not count as the claim.
6. If still missing, list contents with approximate purchase date, value and any proof, honestly and without padding.
7. Check travel insurance, home contents cover and card benefits, and disclose other claims as their terms require.
8. Add follow-up events and log every contact.

## Watch out for
- Throwing away a damaged bag or its tag before inspection is settled.
- Liability limits that make valuables and electronics poorly covered.
- On multi-airline trips, check which carrier takes the report.
- Inflated or invented item values can void the claim.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.

## Done when
A referenced report exists, the written claim is filed within its deadline, and receipts and contents list are attached.
