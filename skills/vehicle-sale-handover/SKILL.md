# Vehicle Sale Handover

## When to use
A vehicle is about to change owner.

## Steps
1. From the official vehicle authority, find the transfer steps: forms, who submits, plates, roadworthiness certificate, tax and any required wording.
2. If finance is outstanding, get the settlement figure and process from the lender before advertising.
3. Gather title or registration, service history, keys and receipts. Hide plates and document numbers in public photos where they could be misused.
4. Describe faults honestly in writing.
5. Allow test drives only after confirming licence and insurance cover; go along, and keep the keys whenever the buyer is not driving.
6. Take payment by a method that can be confirmed as cleared in the seller's own bank. Agree this beforehand.
7. Complete a signed receipt with names, VIN, mileage, price, date, time and condition terms, then submit the ownership transfer.
8. Remove personal data: paired phones, saved addresses, garage door codes, connected-car account and toll tags. Then cancel or move insurance and any subscriptions.

## Watch out for
- Handing over on a payment screenshot or uncleared cheque.
- Fines arriving because the transfer was never filed.
- The old owner's app still able to locate or unlock the car.
- Cancelling insurance while the car is still theirs.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Cleared funds are confirmed, the transfer is acknowledged by the authority or filed with proof, and the old accounts are unlinked.
