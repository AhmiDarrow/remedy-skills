# Move House Plan

## When to use
A move is confirmed or being priced.

## Steps
1. Get the move date and how firm it is, both addresses, renting or owning, distance or border crossing, people, children, pets, budget and who is helping.
2. Check deadlines first: lease notice, completion dates, deposit rules, building move slots, lift booking, parking permit, school and care places.
3. Build an inventory by room, marking keep, sell, donate, dispose. Measure large items against doors, stairs and new rooms.
4. Compare movers or van hire on the same inventory: what is included, insurance cover and limits, cancellation, access charges. Check reviews and registration; be wary of large cash deposits.
5. Date the tasks backward: about two months (book, declutter), two weeks (pack rarely used rooms, confirm), the day before, moving day and the first week. Compress honestly if time is short.
6. Make a service list with date and status: electricity, gas, water, internet, insurance for both homes and goods in transit, mail redirection.
7. Make an address list: employer, banks, tax and government records, vehicle and licence, doctors, schools, pet microchip, subscriptions.
8. Number each box with destination room and contents; keep the list. Carry documents, medicines, keys, valuables and chargers personally, and pack a first-night box.
9. At both ends, record meter readings and condition photos, test alarms, find shut-offs and consider changing locks.

## Watch out for
- Movers refuse some items, often fuel, gas bottles, paint and plants; ask.
- Sending the new address and move date publicly.
- Appliances may need draining, defrosting or a qualified person to disconnect.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
A dated list with owners covers legal deadlines, booking, services, address changes and essentials, with unconfirmed items marked.
