# Contractor Quote Compare

## When to use
Two or more quotes exist, or are about to be requested, and the totals are not directly comparable.

## Steps
1. If quotes are not yet in, write one scope sheet (what, where, materials grade, access, timing) so every firm prices the same job.
2. Extract each quote into a table with one row per scope item. Cells are "priced", "excluded", "provisional" or "not mentioned". Silence is not inclusion.
3. List separately: tax, waste removal, scaffolding, permits and inspections, making good (plaster, paint, flooring), delivery, and what happens if hidden damage is found.
4. Flag provisional sums and day-rate items, and show a low and high total for each quote.
5. Compare terms: payment schedule tied to milestones, deposit size, start and duration, written warranty, how variations are approved.
6. Verify the firm: insurance certificate with expiry date, and any licence or trade registration the work legally needs, checked on the issuer's own register rather than a logo on the quote.
7. Draft one question list per firm covering its "not mentioned" cells. Hand the drafts over for the person to send.
8. After answers, redo the table and summarise trade-offs. The choice stays with the person.

## Watch out for
- A much cheaper total often means a smaller scope; find the missing rows before praising it.
- Large upfront cash payments, pressure to decide today, or a changed bank account by message. Confirm payment details by phone on a number already known.
- Quote validity dates lapsing while questions are open.
- "Estimate" and "quote" may carry different weight; ask whether the price is fixed.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Every scope row is marked for every firm, open items are listed as questions, and each total is shown as fixed or as a range.
