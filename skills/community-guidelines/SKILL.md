# Community Guidelines

## When to use
A community needs written rules, or current ones lead to arguments and uneven enforcement.

## Steps
1. Learn the community's purpose, size, audience including possible minors, spaces covered, moderator coverage and recent incidents.
2. Check the host platform's current terms; local rules cannot permit what it forbids. Legal reporting duties and age requirements need qualified review.
3. Write a few rules about observable behaviour, each with its reason and a borderline example. Cover harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, private information, spam, self-promotion and impersonation as relevant.
4. Define what happens off-platform and in private messages: when it counts.
5. Describe reporting: where, what to include, a path that bypasses a moderator who is the subject, and who sees reports. Give only response times that can be staffed.
6. Set an enforcement ladder with factors such as severity, intent, pattern and risk. Allow immediate removal for severe harm.
7. Write the moderator side: evidence to keep, recusal for conflicts, urgent escalation including emergency services for imminent danger, and message templates.
8. Add appeals reviewed by someone other than the original decider where staffing allows.
9. Test against a dozen hard cases, including past incidents and false reports, and revise where two moderators would decide differently.

## Watch out for
- "Be respectful" as the only rule gives no shared standard.
- Exhaustive lists invite loophole arguments; keep a stated judgement clause.
- Publishing reporter identities or incident details.

## Operating rules
- Look before you change anything: read the real files, data or page first.
- Report what you actually ran and saw; never invent results, sources or numbers.
- Ask before destructive, paid, public or irreversible actions.
- Never print, store or commit secrets or private personal data.

## Done when
Test cases yield consistent outcomes, reporting and appeal paths name who acts, and a one-screen summary exists for newcomers.
